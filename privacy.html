Sanj Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Sanj Studio is an invite-only online studio for planning and managing video work. This page explains, in plain words, what information the studio keeps and why.

Who runs Sanj Studio

Sanj Studio is run by its owner ("the Admin"). You can reach the Admin from inside the studio with the WhatsApp button, or by email at vixxionbaloch@gmail.com.

What we collect

Sign-in information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. We use it only to make your account, show you in the Admin's People list, and keep your own workspace separate from everyone else's.

Your work inside the studio. The things you add or make in the studio — channel links, niches, scripts, characters, voices, jobs, settings, and the account labels you connect — are saved in the studio's database so the studio can do its work for you.

Problem reports. If you use the WhatsApp button or send a report, we receive the message you write.

What we do NOT collect

We never ask for or store your passwords. Sign-in happens only through Google's own pages. The studio stores account labels and email addresses for the accounts you connect — never their passwords or secret keys. We do not sell your information to anyone.

Access and approval

Sanj Studio is invite-only. New accounts stay in a "Waiting" state until the Admin gives access, or the account holder enters the personal access code the Admin gave them. Each signed-in person can see only their own workspace. The Admin can see the People list (names, emails, and account status) to manage access, and can cut off access at any time.

Where data is stored

Account and workspace data is stored in the studio's database (Supabase). The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Google sign-in is provided by Google.

Your choices

You can ask the Admin at any time to see, correct, or delete your account and workspace data. If your access is cut off, your saved work stays yours and is not given to any other person.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
